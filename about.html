About

What are the best things in the world, as voted by people?

Best Things is a simple experiment: you're shown two things, and you pick the one you like better. That's the whole game.

Every vote is anonymous and shared by everyone. Across thousands of random matchups, patterns emerge — and the Leaderboard reveals what the world collectively thinks are the best things.

What counts as a "thing"? Anything almost anyone can have an opinion about — foods, animals, weather, everyday objects, even big abstract ideas like time, love, or gravity. We deliberately leave out groups of people (countries, religions, and the like): this is about things, not about us.

Want a challenge? The Quiz shows you a matchup and asks which one the crowd ranks higher. See how long a streak you can build.

That's it — have fun.

Experiment setup blatantly stolen from Tom Scott's experiment. The quiz is new though!
